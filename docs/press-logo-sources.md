# Homepage publisher marks

Verified 9 October 2026. These links host the EIN Presswire release “Lovepreet Singh Connects Technology and Design at Five Rivers Inc. and Lovelace” (release 948264030). They are press release placements, not independent newsroom endorsements. The homepage uses that explicit description. Selection is based on publisher recognition and confirmed article availability; no article view totals were available.

The original release and AP News, National Law Review and The Leader copies were read in the browser during the distribution audit. WGN-TV and PIX11 copies were read during this update; both identify EIN Presswire as the source and state that the content was not written or endorsed by the station's reporting staff.

Article URLs are maintained in `src/data/pressPlacements.js`. No generic parent-network logos or search engine logos are used.

Logo assets were exported from the images displayed in the public [EIN distribution report](https://www.einpresswire.com/press-releases/report/iCY7sD-tKg7pX3Ff). Original files are retained beside monochrome derivatives. Raster marks were trimmed to their visible bounds and rendered in monochrome for the dark ribbon. SVG marks use a monochrome CSS filter. Publisher trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
