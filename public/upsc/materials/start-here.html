Start your UPSC learning programme

Begin with one understandable lesson, practise it, and save what happened. You do not need a label about your intelligence to get started.

Open the study dashboard · Download the study kit ZIP

Do these three things first

  1. Open the dashboard. Set a realistic daily study budget and language preference. The starter routine is 4 hours shared across study priorities, with simple English and Hindi explanations.
  2. Try the 20-minute starting check, then read Lesson 1. Answer in your own words; “I don't know yet” is a useful response. Start shorter if needed and build up gradually.
  3. Record your actual answers and the next task in the dashboard. Export a JSON backup and an AI handoff after the session. Keep them in storage you control.

The complete plan contains daily routines, the first four weeks, degree-pathway checks, an optional recruitment-exam track and later UPSC phases. Do not buy a large book collection before trying the first lessons.

Keep your progress when accounts change

  1. After each study session, export the latest AI handoff and JSON backup from the dashboard.
  2. Give the handoff to the new AI and say:

Continue as my UPSC mentor using this handoff. Read my profile, actual answers, review queue and next step. Teach one small concept in simple English with Hindi support. Do not assume that reading a lesson means I mastered it. After the session, return a compatible JSON learning update so I can save the progress.

  1. Import or paste the AI's JSON update into the dashboard. Review it for accuracy, then export a fresh handoff and backup.

A mentor with file access may save to a private location you provide. Without file access, the mentor must give you the update to save. No AI account or application automatically knows what another one taught you.

Your study records stay in this browser on this device. The public website supplies the dashboard and learning materials; it is not a private cloud account for your progress. Changing devices, clearing browser storage, using a private window or opening a different site address can mean your records are unavailable. Import the latest backup to continue elsewhere. If storage is unavailable, export before closing the page.

Keep one backup on your own device and another in a private place you choose. A handoff contains your learning history: share it only with a mentor or service you choose. Never add personal handoffs, answers or JSON backups to the public website, a public repository or a public download folder. The public kit includes no learner profile or personal progress file.

Put eligibility and your degree on the right track

If you do not yet have the required qualification, plan a suitable recognised bachelor's degree while building foundations. If you already have a degree, check the actual award and your other eligibility conditions against the relevant official notice. BA is not compulsory; choose a course you can finish and use beyond UPSC.

For a regular college, verify the awarding institution and relevant affiliation. For distance/online study, check the exact institution, programme, mode and admission session through the applicable UGC/UGC-DEB records. Read the real degree-award requirements, fees and examination arrangements before enrolling.

Being in the second year of a degree does not automatically establish CSE eligibility. The normal degree requirement and any conditional qualifying-exam/final-year/result-awaited provision depend on the exam-year notice and its proof deadline. A planned course is not a completed qualification. Choose an attempt window only after your academic timetable and eligibility are clear.

If employment is an immediate goal, the plan includes an optional track for one suitable Class 12-level recruitment examination. Check the actual notification before deciding eligibility or applying. Use shared arithmetic, reasoning and reading skills, then fit the target's specific practice inside the same study budget.

Use evidence, not streaks

Reading is exposure. A correct answer after seeing a solution is assisted practice. An independent explanation or application gives better evidence of learning. Record those distinctions honestly. Review a new concept after roughly 1, 3, 7 and 21 days, then adjust the gaps to actual recall.

The exam guide identifies official sources and verification limits. Check the applicable official notification and corrigenda before relying on dates, eligibility or application rules. This programme offers a way to learn and preserve progress; it does not guarantee selection.
